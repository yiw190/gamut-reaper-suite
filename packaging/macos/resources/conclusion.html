Step 3 — restart REAPER

Quit REAPER and open it again. From now on the bridge starts with it and the Hibiki panel opens docked on the right. Click the agent name at the top of the panel to pick a provider and model, then send your first message.

Step 4 — connect an agent

Gamut (desktop app): download for Mac and sign in with your Gamut account, then click Enable REAPER for your agent in the Hibiki panel.
Or a CLI agent: run hibiki agents add claude kimi in Terminal, then click Connect in the panel.

Check everything in Terminal:

hibiki doctor

Optional: hibiki agents add codex claude kimi adds those agents to the panel. Remove with hibiki uninstall. Version @VERSION@.
